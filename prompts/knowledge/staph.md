**Staphylococcus aureus** `[src: Berryhill 2024, Kim 2024; unverified where noted]` — 2nd most common
clinical target (~40% of cases).
- **Receptors (Gram-positive):** **wall teichoic acid (WTA)** + glycosylation and peptidoglycan are
  the usual *phage* receptors `[src: unverified]`. Note teichoic acid is also the **host innate-immune
  recognition ligand** (hemocyte/phagocyte uptake) — don't conflate the two roles `[src: Berryhill 2024]`.
  Many *S. aureus* phages are broad-host **Herelleviridae** (e.g. Kayvirus).
- **Immunity is often decisive** (Galleria model): outcome is inoculum-dependent with a **critical
  density ~1e6 CFU** above which control fails by **immune-effector exhaustion** (not just saturation);
  the immune pool is finite and must activate + engage before killing `[src: Berryhill 2024]`. Innate
  immunity also **suppresses invasion-when-rare of resistant minorities in vivo** (they sweep in vitro
  but not in the host) — an immune effect distinct from the mutant's intrinsic fitness cost.
- **Treatment ≠ sterilisation.** Phage *and* every antibiotic tested pushed density only to ~the
  critical threshold and left a **persistent protected refuge** — model as threshold-crossing + immune
  finish with a residual pool `[src: Berryhill 2024]`; **SCVs / biofilm** are the refuge (map: dormancy,
  see [[persisters_dormancy]]).
- **Antibiotic partners:** **daptomycin confirmed effective** (bactericidal) `[src: Berryhill 2024]`;
  **vancomycin** synergistic with a Staph cocktail `[src: Kim 2024]`; oxacillin (MSSA). **Caution:
  rifampin is antagonistic with *S. aureus* phages** (interferes with the phage replication cycle) even
  though it *synergises* with *P. aeruginosa* phages — antibiotic-interaction sign is organism-specific
  `[src: Kim 2024]`.
